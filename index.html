How to Play

  1. Enter your name and add a photo (camera or file). Press Start Game.
  2. Follow the row‑wise (serpentine) path—arrows show direction.
  3. Coins add points; thieves subtract points.
  4. Exact finish: You must roll the exact number to reach Finish. If you roll more than needed, your turn ends—try again next roll.
  5. If points drop below 0, you return to Start (points reset to 0).
  6. Win with a Gold Pair. Final score = Points + Rolls. Victory music plays and the scoreboard updates.
