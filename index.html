How to Play

  1. Enter your name. A sample avatar is shown by default; optionally capture a photo (camera) or choose a file.
  2. Follow the row‑wise (serpentine) path—arrows show direction. Rows are color‑coded to guide the path.
  3. Coins add points (🪙 +3, 🪙 +2, 🪙 +1); thieves subtract points (🎣 −2, 🗡️ −3, 🪝 −16).
  4. Exact finish: Roll the exact number to reach Finish.
  5. If points drop below 0, you return to Start (points reset to 0).
  6. Win with a Gold Pair. Final score = Points + Rolls. Victory music plays and the scoreboard updates.
